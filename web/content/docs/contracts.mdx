## Pons V2 contracts

<ContractList group="pons" />

Pons' contracts are verified on Robinhood Chain's Blockscout. Each coin also has its own launch curve contract, created by the factory at launch; its address is on the coin's page.

Every coin is its own contract too, created by Pons: an ERC-20 with 18 decimals and a fixed supply of {PARAMS.supply}, minted once, straight to its launch curve. Its name, ticker, logo, description and links are stored in it. It has no owner and no mint function; any holder can burn their own coins.

## Who controls what

### twain

The twain launcher has an owner: twain. Ownership can be handed over in two steps, but never renounced. The owner can do exactly two things:

- pause and resume new launches through twain;
- change the address that receives twain's {NUM.twainSplit} of each vault's income.

The owner cannot touch any coin, curve, pool or locked liquidity, or any fees owed to a creator. Fee vaults have no owner at all, and no twain contract can be upgraded.

### Pons

Pons V2 has its own owner, a multisig. For **new** launches, Pons can change its launch fee, the curve terms and the list of pair assets. For coins already launched, it cannot change their fees, tax or pool, cannot pause trading, and cannot move locked liquidity.

It **can** redirect a coin's creator fee recipient, its twain fee vault, to another address. Such a change takes effect only after a public 3-day timelock. Pons has used this power before, for example at creators' request. While a change is pending for a twain coin, the coin's page shows a warning for those 3 days. Fees the vault has already received stay claimable.

### Uniswap

Uniswap v4's contracts are immutable.

These limits hold at the contract level. They rely on Robinhood Chain itself, and on the contract of each pair asset, which are covered in [Risks](/docs/risks).
