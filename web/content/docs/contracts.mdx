The launchpad is two contracts on Robinhood Chain, plus one coin contract per launch. None of them is a proxy, so none can be upgraded. The source is verified: open any address below to read it.

## Launchpad contracts

Every coin is its own contract: a standard ERC-20 with 18 decimals and a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, minted once, straight to the locker. It has no owner and no mint or burn function. Its address is on the coin's page.

## Uniswap v4 on Robinhood Chain

Every coin trades in a Uniswap v4 pool. These are Uniswap's own contracts, used as they are.

## Admin powers

The launchpad has an owner. Ownership can be handed over in two steps, but never renounced. The owner can do exactly three things:

- change the treasury address that receives the protocol's share of fees;
- pause and resume the creation of **new** coins;
- add assets to the list new coins can be paired with, change their start market cap, or take them off the list — again for **new** coins only.

The owner cannot:

- stop or pause trading in any pool;
- change any parameter of a coin that is live, such as its supply, pair, start price or fees;
- touch a coin's liquidity, a creator's unclaimed fees, or the locked positions;
- upgrade any contract.

The locker has no owner at all.

These guarantees hold at the contract level. They rely on Robinhood Chain itself, and on the contracts of each paired asset, which are covered in [Risks](/docs/risks).
