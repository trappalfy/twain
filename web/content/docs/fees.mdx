Every trade of a twain coin pays {PARAMS.tradeFeePct} of its value, on the launch curve and in the Uniswap pool alike: a {PARAMS.ponsFeePct} Pons fee and a {PARAMS.creatorTaxPct} creator tax. The rates are set when the coin launches, they are the same for every twain coin, and nobody can change them for a coin that is live.

There is no fee to twain for launching. Pons charges a small launch fee in ETH, shown in the form before you sign.

## Where it goes

<FeeSplit />

Pons keeps {NUM.ponsKeepsOfFee} of its fee. The other {NUM.vaultGetsOfFee}, and the whole creator tax, go to the coin's fee vault: {NUM.vault} of trading volume. The vault splits everything it receives {NUM.creatorSplit} to the creator and {NUM.twainSplit} to twain.

The snipe tax on buys in the first {PARAMS.snipeWindow} is added to the Pons fee and split the same way.

## In which currency

Fees are taken from the asset side of every trade: a buy pays them out of the asset it brings, a sell out of the asset it receives. So fees are always paid in the coin's pair asset: ETH for a coin paired with ETH, a stock token for a coin paired with that stock.

## The fee vault

Each coin has its own fee vault, a small twain contract created in the launch transaction. At Pons it is the coin's creator fee recipient: Pons credits the creator's share of every trade to it. The vault has no owner and no upgrade path. It keeps the creator's balance and twain's balance apart, and pays each side only its own.

## Claiming

Creators claim from [Profile → Creator fees](/profile#creator-fees) or from the coin's page. A claim pulls in what Pons has credited to the vault, splits it, and pays the creator in one transaction, each coin in its own pair asset.

Anyone may trigger a claim, but the money always goes to the creator, never to whoever sent the transaction.

A creator can hand the role to another address by calling `transferCreator` on the coin's vault. Unclaimed fees, and all future ones, go with it.

## What that adds up to

<FeeExamples />

A coin that people keep trading keeps paying its creator. The best thing a creator can do is stay, and that is the behaviour these fees reward.

<Callout title="Where twain's share goes">

twain's {NUM.twainSplit} is paid to a treasury address set on the twain launcher. twain's owner can change that address, but not any rate, split or vault. Pons can redirect a coin's creator fee recipient after a public 3-day notice; see [Contracts](/docs/contracts#who-controls-what).

</Callout>
