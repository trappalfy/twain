This is a coin launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Anyone can launch a coin here and pair it with an asset from the list: ETH, a tokenized stock, or another token. Every coin is launched under the same rules, written into the contracts before the first one shipped. Nobody can change them for a coin that is already live.

## The life of a coin

1. **Launch.** A creator picks a name, a ticker, an image and the [asset to pair with](/docs/paired-assets), and signs one transaction. In that transaction the contract mints 1,000,000,000 coins, opens the coin's own Uniswap v4 pool against the asset, and puts the whole supply into it. There is no launch fee, only gas.
2. **The first buy.** The creator can buy first, in the same transaction, at the pool's start price. Nobody can get in before that buy.
3. **Trading.** From the next block on, the coin trades in its pool like any Uniswap pair: on this site, through aggregators and bots, or on Uniswap itself. [The price](/docs/the-price) rises with every buy and falls with every sell.
4. **The lock.** The pool's liquidity is [locked for good](/docs/liquidity-lock). It earns 1% of every trade, [split 60/40](/docs/fees) between the creator and the protocol for as long as the coin trades.

## One supply, no allocations

Every coin is minted with 1,000,000,000 units, and all of them go into the pool. Nothing else: no presale, no team share, no allocation to anyone.

A creator who wants to hold their own coin buys it from the pool, at the pool's price, like everyone else.

## What the launchpad does and does not do

You sign every transaction from your own wallet, and the site never holds your keys. Each coin's liquidity and any unclaimed fees sit in the contracts, under the rules on these pages, not in an account anyone can move.

<Callout title="The rest is up to the coin">

A launchpad can't make a coin good. What it can do is make the launch the same for everyone: same supply, same pool, same lock, same split. After that, the coin belongs to whoever builds around it.

</Callout>
