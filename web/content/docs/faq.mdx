## Launching

<QuestionList>

<Question q="What does it cost to launch a coin?">

Gas only. There is no launch fee. If you also buy your own coin in the launch transaction, that buy is a normal pool buy with the 1% fee.

</Question>

<Question q="Which asset should I pair my coin with?">

Any asset on the list works the same way. The pair decides what buyers pay with and what your fees are paid in: a coin paired with a stock token earns its fees in that stock token. See [Paired assets](/docs/paired-assets).

</Question>

<Question q="Can a creator get coins for free?">

No. There are no allocations of any kind. A creator who wants to hold their coin buys it from the pool, at the pool's price, like everyone else. The launch lets the creator buy first, in the same transaction.

</Question>

<Question q="Can I change the name, ticker, supply or pair after launch?">

No. The name, ticker and supply are fixed in the coin's contract, and the pair is fixed by its pool.

</Question>

</QuestionList>

## Buying and selling

<QuestionList>

<Question q="Where can I trade a coin?">

Anywhere that trades Uniswap v4: this site, aggregators, wallets, bots and the Uniswap app. The pool exists from the launch transaction on.

</Question>

<Question q="Why do I approve twice when I pay with a token?">

Uniswap takes tokens through its Permit2 contract. The first approval lets Permit2 handle the token, once; the second lets Uniswap's router spend this amount. Paying with native ETH needs neither.

</Question>

<Question q="What does a price like 0.0₇393 ETH mean?">

The small number counts the zeros after the decimal point. 0.0₇393 ETH is 0.0000000393 ETH.

</Question>

<Question q="How is market cap calculated?">

Price times the full supply of 1,000,000,000, in the coin's asset. Dollar values use the asset's current price and are approximate.

</Question>

</QuestionList>

## The pool

<QuestionList>

<Question q="Can anyone remove the liquidity?">

No. The position belongs to the locker, which can only collect fees. It has no withdraw function, no owner, and cannot be upgraded. See [Liquidity lock](/docs/liquidity-lock).

</Question>

<Question q="Can the price go below the start?">

Not through the launch liquidity: it starts at the start price, so selling every coin bought from the pool brings the price back to the start, not lower. Anyone can add their own liquidity to a Uniswap pool at any price, and only that could take it lower. The price in dollars can also fall if the paired asset falls.

</Question>

</QuestionList>

## Fees and rules

<QuestionList>

<Question q="How do creators claim their fees?">

From [Profile → Creator fees](/profile#creator-fees) or the coin's page. A claim collects the fees still in the pool and pays them in one transaction.

</Question>

<Question q="Can the fees or the rules change?">

No. Every parameter is fixed in the contracts. The owner can only change the treasury address, pause the creation of new coins and manage the asset list for new coins. See [Admin powers](/docs/contracts#admin-powers).

</Question>

<Question q="Can anyone stop me from selling?">

No contract function here can pause trading. Robinhood Chain's operator runs the sequencer and can delay or filter transactions, and an asset's issuer controls that asset's contract; see [Risks](/docs/risks).

</Question>
