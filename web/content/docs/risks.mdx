## Coins are made by users

Anyone can launch a coin here. Nobody reviews, selects or endorses them. A name, ticker or image can imitate another project. Most coins lose value, and any coin can go to zero. Buy only what you can afford to lose entirely.

## Smart-contract risk

The contracts can contain bugs that nobody has found yet. Because they cannot be upgraded, a bug also cannot be patched in place. The same property that keeps the rules fixed keeps any flaw fixed.

Every coin trades in a Uniswap v4 pool, so it also depends on Uniswap's contracts.

## The paired asset

A coin is priced in its asset. When the asset's price falls, the coin's dollar value falls with it, even if nobody trades the coin.

Stock tokens and other listed tokens are run by their issuers. An issuer can upgrade its token, pause it or block addresses. If an issuer blocked the Uniswap PoolManager, or the launchpad, the pools and fee balances in that token could stop moving. Stock tokens follow their stock's price, not a share: they are not the stock itself. Memecoins can lose most of their value or their liquidity quickly; a coin paired with one carries that risk too, and it keeps its pair even if the token is later taken off the list.

## The chain underneath

Every guarantee on these pages holds at the contract level, and depends on Robinhood Chain. The chain's sequencer is run by the chain operator, which orders transactions and can delay or filter them. If the sequencer stops or filters a transaction, trades may not go through when you expect, or at all.

## Market risk

Prices move with every trade, and fast. The pool rewards early buyers: later buyers pay more for the same number of coins, and selling back pays less than buying cost, after 1% each way. There is no limit on how much one buyer can take right after a launch; bots can and do buy in the first blocks. Slippage protects you from a price worse than you accepted, not from a falling price.

## Your wallet, your transactions

You sign every transaction yourself, and a confirmed transaction cannot be undone. The site never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Check the address in your browser before you connect.

## Figures on the site

Charts, prices and totals come from the site's indexer of onchain events and can lag behind the chain for a few seconds. Dollar values use each asset's current price. The contracts are the source of truth, and every transaction can be checked on Blockscout.
